## Screen coverage

- **Home:** explains safe and legal migration, plans, documents, recruitment, support, and what public submission does. The reference number and staff review are app mechanics; the booklet does not promise those outcomes.
- **Booklet reader:** uses the source's cover title and describes its main sections without reproducing its changing contact listings or detailed rules.
- **Public intake:** collects stage and purpose, preparation status, recruitment route, safety concerns, and support needs with booklet-aligned labels. Consent and privacy text describes the actual app service.
- **Review and staff portal:** shows submitted answers with the same localized labels instead of raw stored values. Operational statuses such as “submitted” and “under review” describe case work, not migration authorization.
- **Privacy, sign-in, and account help:** retain service-specific language. The booklet does not define the app's authentication, consent, data handling, or submission process.

## Editorial boundaries

Use short booklet terms and the booklet's topic sequence. Correct spelling and grammar where needed. Avoid copying claims that may stigmatize people, promise a result, or depend on changing law, official contacts, travel rules, or external websites. Do not turn a reported concern into a finding of illegality, fraud, or danger. Current official referral details require verification before publication.
